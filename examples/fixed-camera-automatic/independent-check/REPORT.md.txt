## Independent visual observations and limitations

1. All 30 accepted white boxes were visually inspected in accepted-white-1.jpg through accepted-white-3.jpg against source-derived frames. They consistently enclose the intended white T-shirt/dark trousers/pale shoes. No wrong-person accepted white observation was seen in this inspected set. This is a small draft audit, not an accuracy estimate.
2. Recall is limited even around the chosen reference. White is plainly visible at 12, 13.4, 15, 18 and 27 seconds in court-audit-1.jpg, but all early candidate windows remain uncertain. At the exact 13.4-second reference, the corresponding detection f00067_p00 is uncertain with insufficient_contiguous_texture_support; 13.6 and 14.0 have the same reason. At 12 seconds the reason is texture_distance_too_large. This is conservative behavior, but an operator cannot assume reference-adjacent or plainly visible appearances will be recovered.
3. Uncertain best-profile labels can be wrong. At 8 seconds, the visible white player is labelled green?; at 50 seconds the full white-person box f00250_p00 is green?; at 60 seconds the white player f00300_p01 is green?. These do not enter the accepted or high group. Wrong tentative profile IDs should not be presented as recognized identities.
4. At 50 seconds, the detector also emits a narrow overlapping partial box f00250_p01 over the white player (roughly x384–396, y422–481), plus a tiny edge proposal. The matching reasons include ambiguous_same_frame_person/quality failures. Duplicate partial detections can therefore reduce support; this is a concrete difficult input, not evidence that white is absent.
5. supported_time_ranges is a segment envelope, not uninterrupted accepted identity at every sampled instant. The 55.8–58.6 segment includes a 57.6→58.2 accepted-observation gap. The 58.0 box remains explicitly uncertain (quality and texture distance). No identity was propagated to that rejected box, but a consumer reading only the interval boundaries could overstate continuous support. Retain the per-observation record when describing this interval.
6. The two high rough windows overlap by two seconds, so duplicate source content remains in this coverage-based review. The current task stopped before editing; deduplication is an editorial review question.
7. The portrait source is pillarboxed into 1280×720 rough media. The whole image is preserved, but distant players are very small. The extracted source/court/crop evidence is more useful for identity review than the landscape rough alone.

Warnings in logs about unavailable xFormers and ORT telemetry persistence did not prevent successful local matching or rendering. No CLI crash occurred.
